## SIPOC Summary

This SIPOC captures the full span of the employee offboarding process at a large automotive manufacturing company — from the moment a separation is initiated through the 30-day post-departure close-out — involving eight cross-functional process steps and key suppliers including HR, IT, Payroll, Plant Operations, and Legal. The primary customers of this process are both internal stakeholders — such as hiring managers, compliance teams, and remaining team members who depend on timely handoffs and accurate documentation — and the departing employee, whose final experience directly influences employer brand and potential alumni re-engagement. By mapping suppliers, inputs, outputs, and customers at each step, this SIPOC establishes the foundation for identifying process gaps, clarifying ownership, and prioritizing improvement opportunities in the redesign effort.
