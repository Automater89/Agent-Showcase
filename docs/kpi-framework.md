## KPI Table

| KPI Name | What It Measures | Target | Data Source | Review Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Offboarding Cycle Time** | Average number of business days from separation notification to full case closure (all tasks complete, record terminated in HRIS) | ≤ 5 business days for standard separations | HRIS / Offboarding Case Dashboard | Monthly | HR Coordinator |
| **IT Access Revocation Timeliness** | Percentage of offboarding cases where all system access is fully revoked by end-of-business on the employee's last day | ≥ 95% on-time revocation | IT Service Management System / Deprovisioning Audit Log | Monthly | IT Admin |
| **Knowledge Transfer Completion Rate** | Percentage of offboarding cases where the knowledge transfer checklist is marked ≥ 80% complete before the employee's last day | ≥ 85% of cases meet the threshold | Offboarding Portal / Power Apps Dashboard | Monthly | HR Coordinator / Manager |
| **Exit Interview Completion Rate** | Percentage of departing employees who submit a completed digital exit interview survey | ≥ 70% submission rate | Exit Survey Platform / HRIS | Monthly | HR Coordinator |
| **Final Pay Error Rate** | Percentage of final paychecks that require a correction or generate an employee dispute after issuance | ≤ 2% error rate | Payroll System / HR Case Log | Monthly | Payroll / HR Coordinator |
| **Manager Satisfaction Score (Offboarding Support)** | Manager-reported satisfaction with the offboarding tools, guidance, and support they received during the process (post-case survey, 1–5 scale) | Average score ≥ 4.0 / 5.0 | Post-Case Manager Survey (automated) | Quarterly | HR Coordinator |
| **Departing Employee Experience Score** | Departing employee-reported satisfaction with the offboarding experience (exit survey question, 1–5 scale) | Average score ≥ 3.8 / 5.0 | Exit Interview Survey | Quarterly | HR Leadership |
| **Top Exit Theme Detection Lag** | Number of days between a turnover theme emerging in exit interviews and it being surfaced to HR leadership in a report | ≤ 30 days from trend emergence to leadership visibility | AI Exit Interview Dashboard / Monthly Report | Quarterly (trend review) | HR Leadership / Workforce Analytics |

## How AI and Automation Change the Measurement Story

In the current state, most of these KPIs are either unmeasured or measured manually — cycle times are estimated from email timestamps, exit interview themes are identified by individual HR partners reading through notes, and access revocation compliance is essentially unknown because there is no audit trail. The redesigned process fundamentally changes what is measurable and how fast: Power Automate generates timestamped audit logs for every task, making cycle time and timeliness KPIs automatically calculable without manual data collection; AI classification of exit interview responses compresses the detection lag for emerging turnover themes from months (or never) to days, enabling HR leadership to act on signals before they become systemic problems. The shift is not simply one of efficiency — it is a shift from a process that was largely invisible to leadership into one that is continuously monitored, objectively scored, and capable of generating proactive alerts when performance drifts outside acceptable thresholds.
